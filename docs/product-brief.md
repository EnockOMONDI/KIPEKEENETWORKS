# Kipekee Networks Product Brief

Kipekee Networks is a product by Kipekee Studio.

Tagline: **Hire AI employees trained on your business.**

Kipekee Networks helps African businesses hire digital employees that understand their business documents, workflows, policies, customers, and tools.

Customers do not see Hermes, choose models, buy tokens, or manage prompts. They log into Kipekee Networks and work with AI employees.

## Business Model

Kipekee Networks has two revenue layers:

1. Setup/onboarding fee
2. Monthly user subscription

Monthly subscription: **KES 4,500 per user per month**

## Onboarding Packages

| Package | Setup Fee | Includes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Personal Setup | KES 5,000 | 4 AI employees, support, document memory |
| Business Starter | KES 15,000 | 8 AI employees, support, loops, document memory |
| Max Onboarding | KES 50,000 | Advanced setup, priority support, deeper workflow setup |
| Custom Workflow / Integration | Quoted separately | CRM, WhatsApp, email, calendar, accounting, custom automation |

## Company Structure

Kipekee Studio is the parent company and the first customer.

Kipekee Networks is the AI workforce platform.

Hermes is the hidden orchestration layer for profiles, skills, tools, model routing, cron, memory, and execution.

## Core Rule

AI employee templates are reusable. Company employee instances are isolated.

The same Marketing Manager template can be used by many companies, but each company has separate memory, documents, sessions, permissions, and audit logs.
